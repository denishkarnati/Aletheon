## What this shows

The address claim passes but is flagged weak: it rests only on a community mirror. To make it strong, index ST's own CMSIS pack (`index_svd` with a .pack and `entry`) or cross-check against the reference manual (RM0368). The voltage claim shows the audit doing its job: citing a nearby passage is not enough, the cited text has to contain the value.
