# Code of conduct

We want Aletheon to be a place where hardware and software people of every background can do careful work together.

- Be respectful. Disagree with ideas, not people.
- Assume good faith, and ask before assuming a mistake is carelessness. Firmware is hard.
- No harassment, discrimination, personal attacks, or sharing of others' private information.
- Give credit. Do not present others' work as yours.
- Keep reports of wrong answers factual: what was asked, what came back, what the evidence says.

Maintainers may remove comments, close issues, or ban contributors who break these rules. Report problems privately through the repository's
security/advisory contact or by messaging a maintainer; reports are kept confidential.
