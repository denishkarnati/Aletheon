Where this data comes from

Board and chip specifications come from the open PlatformIO platform repositories (Apache-2.0), read as published: build.mcu, build.cpu, build.f_cpu, upload.maximum_size (flash), upload.maximum_ram_size (RAM), connectivity, frameworks and debug.*. Nothing was estimated or filled in. Core names for boards without a cpu field are derived from the platform and chip family (AVR, ESP32 families, RISC-V, MIPS) and are labelled as such. Silicon vendor is derived from the chip's part-number prefix.

A "register map" flag means a CMSIS-SVD file for that chip exists in the community mirror cmsis-svd/cmsis-svd-data (file list only, no file contents were read). Those files are community-mirror trust class. "basis: manifest" means PlatformIO's own debug manifest names that file; "basis: name" means it was matched by part number. Always check a spec against the vendor's datasheet before you design with it.
