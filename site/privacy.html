Privacy and legal

A plain-language description of this website as it is today. It is not legal advice and it is not a contract.

What this website collects

Nothing, by us. In concrete terms, this site:

  • has no accounts, sign-up forms or email collection;
  • sets no cookies and stores nothing in your browser;
  • loads no analytics, advertising, tracking pixels or session-recording tools;
  • loads no third-party scripts or fonts: the fonts are the ones already installed on your device, and every script and file comes from this same site;
  • runs the Device Lab, the claim auditor, the Chip Atlas and the Parts catalog entirely in your browser. What you type there, including code you write in the Device Lab, is not sent to us.

Like any web host, whoever serves these pages can see your IP address and which pages you requested, in the host's own access logs. We do not receive, combine or analyse those logs. Links on this site lead to other websites (vendor sites, GitHub); they have their own privacy practices.

Aletheon Cloud (not open yet)

A hosted service with accounts, teams and shared evidence is in development. It will have its own privacy notice, terms and age requirements, published before it opens, and this page will be updated then. Nothing on this page describes that service.

Part data, names and datasheets

  • The part list in the Parts catalog is a modified excerpt of the KiCad symbol libraries, licensed CC BY-SA 4.0. See PARTS-DATA-LICENSE.md for what was changed.
  • Device models are written in our own words from public datasheets, with short quotes (never more than 15 words) and the page they come from. Datasheet PDFs and figures are not copied or hosted here; links open the manufacturer's own copy.
  • Part names, company names and logos belong to their owners. Aletheon is independent and is not affiliated with, endorsed by or sponsored by Texas Instruments, Analog Devices, or any other company named here.
  • Manufacturer contact links are public company pages only. We list no personal names, emails or phone numbers.

Accuracy

The data is provided as is. Registers, addresses, voltages and formulas can be wrong or out of date, and the simulator is a behavioral model, not a circuit simulation. Always check the manufacturer's current datasheet before you design, build or order anything.

Contact

For privacy questions, corrections or takedown requests, open an issue at the project's GitHub issue tracker.
